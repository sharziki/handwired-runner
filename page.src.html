Handwired Runner

Runner A is a neural network whose every weight I wrote by hand: eight neurons designed from the jump physics, with no training at all. Runner B is a PPO agent that learned level 01 by trial and error. Both see the same 10 × 13 tile window and press the same four buttons. The idea comes from Weight-Space Programming: weights as a program you can read and write.
